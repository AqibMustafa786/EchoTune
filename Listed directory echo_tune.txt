# EchoTune Product Documentation

## 📱 Core App Features
EchoTune is a premium, high-performance offline music and video player designed with a modern glassmorphic aesthetic.

### 1. Media Library & Management
*   **Offline Playback**: High-fidelity playback for local MP3, WAV, FLAC, and other audio formats.
*   **Video Gallery**: A dedicated section for gallery videos with high-quality thumbnail previews.
*   **Automatic Scanning**: Intelligently scans device storage to organize music by Songs, Albums, and Artists.
*   **Duplicate Cleaner**: A specialized tool to find and remove duplicate audio files to save storage.
*   **Smart Playlists**: Automatically generated lists for *Recently Played*, *Most Played*, and *Recently Added*.

### 2. "Swipe" Technology (Shorts Mode)
EchoTune features a TikTok/Instagram-style vertical swipe interaction within the **Video Player**.
*   **Vertical Swiping**: Effortlessly skip to the next or previous video with a smooth vertical flick.
*   **Haptic Feedback**: High-quality haptics during swipes for a premium tactile feel.
*   **Immersive Fullscreen**: Double-tap to enter a distraction-free immersive mode.
*   **Gestural Controls**: Support for both horizontal and vertical drag gestures to control playback and navigation.

### 3. Professional Equalizer
A sophisticated 5-band equalizer with advanced audio modification tools.
*   **5-Band EQ**: Precise control over 60Hz, 230Hz, 910Hz, 3.6kHz, and 14kHz frequencies.
*   **15+ Presets**: Including Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, Heavy Metal, Hip Hop, Dance, Bass Boost, Treble Boost, Vocal, and Electronic.
*   **Deep Bass Boost**: A dedicated slider to enhance low-end frequencies (0–100%).
*   **3D Surround (Virtualizer)**: Creates an immersive spatial audio experience (0–100%).
*   **Visualizer Card**: Real-time visual feedback of the audio spectrum.

### 4. Moods Feature
Personalize your music collection by tagging songs with emotional moods.
*   **Party 🥳**: High energy for celebrations.
*   **Sad 😔**: Melancholic tracks for reflection.
*   **Chill 😌**: Relaxing ambient beats.
*   **Workout 💪**: High-BPM tracks to keep you moving.
*   **Love ❤️**: Romantic and soulful melodies.
*   **Mood Filtering**: Easily find music that matches your current vibe.
